# Run a retro (60 min)

1. [stated] Send one question the day before: "What surprised you this sprint?"
2. [stated] Open with a two-minute silent write, not a round of talking.
3. [stated] Cluster the notes on the wall together; don't let one person sort.
4. [stated] Pick at most two themes by dot vote; park the rest visibly.
5. [stated] For each theme, agree one experiment with an owner and a check-in date.
6. [stated] Close with a one-word round; skip it if the room is tired.
7. [stated] Send the photo of the wall and the two experiments within 24 hours.
