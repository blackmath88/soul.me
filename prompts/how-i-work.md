# "How I work" note (self-authored)

The preferred source (D-014): written by you, on your own time, from your own head.
It carries your working patterns without touching anyone's data. Save it as `vault/data/<date>-self-note.md`.

Write freely: bullets, half-sentences, German or English. Aim for 20 minutes, not perfection.
Skip any question that doesn't fit.

## Questions

**Who I am at work**
1. What do you do, in one sentence a stranger would understand?
2. What have you done before that still shapes how you work?
3. What are you working toward in the next year or two?
4. Name two or three things you believe about your field that colleagues would disagree with.

**How to work with me**
5. In which language and format do you want answers? How long?
6. How do you like to plan: a big plan first, or short loops?
7. What makes you trust an answer? What makes you stop trusting it?
8. What do you push back on most often?
9. What should an assistant never do with you?

**What I carry**
10. Which projects or responsibilities are current, and until when?
11. Which procedures do you repeat often enough that you could write down the steps?
12. Which tools do you rely on, and how do you like them set up?

**Tensions**
13. Where do you contradict yourself? For example, wanting structure but hating rigid agendas.
