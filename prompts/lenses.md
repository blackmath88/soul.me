# Lenses

The questions `tools/extract.py` asks of your conversations. Each `##` section is one lens:
its id, the vault file its findings usually belong in, and the question. Add, remove or reword
lenses freely; the script reads this file on every run.

## how-i-work
target: minime.md#Operating manual
question: How does this person like to work, with an assistant and with others? Language, format, length, planning style, what makes them trust an answer, what they push back on, what they don't want.

## what-i-learned
target: topics/learning.md
question: What has this person learned, realised or changed their mind about? Skills picked up, lessons drawn from mistakes, views that shifted.

## what-i-built
target: areas/
question: What has this person built, shipped, written or run? Projects, products, workshops, documents, with rough dates where the messages show them.

## career
target: minime.md#Identity
question: What is this person's role, background and direction? Jobs, responsibilities, the kind of work they want more or less of.

## procedures
target: skills/
question: Which things does this person do repeatedly, the same way each time? Describe each as a short procedure with steps.

## admin
target: topics/admin.md
question: Which recurring personal-admin patterns does this person have (deadlines, renewals, routines)? Patterns only: never amounts, account numbers, IDs, health details or anything about other people.
